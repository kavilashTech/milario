Healthy Millet Pancakes

A fluffy and nutritious pancake made using ancient grains.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup millet flour
  • 1 egg (or vegan substitute)
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Mix all dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Add milk, egg and vanilla essence. Stir until smooth.
  3. Preheat a pan on low flame and pour batter.
  4. Cook until bubbles form. Flip and cook until golden.
  5. Serve warm with fruits or honey.
